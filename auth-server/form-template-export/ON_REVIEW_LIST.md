# Ontario forms — review list

The 90 templates added in the 2026-08 batch. **The 45 Ontario templates
shipped before it and the 43 BC ones are not in this list and were not
touched** — they are already reviewed and approved.

`Source` is where the field boxes came from. **AcroForm** boxes are the
government's own widget rectangles and only moved vertically, to sit on
their printed rule. **Word** and **XFA** boxes were inferred from the
printed page, so their widths moved too — review those first.

`Flags` is what `check_seating.py` still reports; blank means it is clean.
